HOW TO PICK

01

Press gently

Use your palm, not your fingertips.

Hold the avocado in your palm and apply gentle, even pressure. A ripe avocado should still feel firm, but it will yield slightly.

02

Match it to your timing

Choose a firmer avocado when you have a few days, and one with a gentle give when you plan to eat it now.

The ripest avocado isn't always the best choice.

03

Cosmetic mark or real damage?

Pay more attention to deep dents, localized soft pockets, or areas much softer than the rest.

Don't confuse ugly with damaged.

Inspect the surface detail

04

Check the shape

Natural variation is normal; look for collapse or structural damage.

05

Check around the stem

Look at the skin around the stem for signs of damage or decay.

Leave the stem attached. Its brown color alone is not a spoilage test. Check ripeness with your palm: firm with a little give is ready; very firm needs time.

FAQ

Is color enough to tell whether an avocado is ripe?

No. Hass avocados often darken as they ripen, but firmness is the better final check. Choose one that is still firm and yields slightly to gentle palm pressure.

Should I avoid an avocado with a small surface scuff?

Not necessarily. A minor scuff can be cosmetic. Avoid deeper dents, localized soft pockets, or signs of structural collapse instead.
