==================================================
EUROPEAN PEAR PATH
==================================================

Create a strong section:

CHECK THE NECK.

Teach:

1. Hold the pear gently.
2. Use light thumb pressure just below the stem.
3. Do not squeeze the middle.
4. A slight give at the neck means ready to eat.

This is the flagship interaction.

==================================================
BARTLETT
==================================================

Core rule:

COLOR HELPS.

GREEN
→ YELLOW / GOLDEN

Still use neck feel as confirmation.

Do NOT teach:

==================================================
ANJOU
==================================================

Core rule:

DON'T WAIT FOR YELLOW.

Anjou does not dramatically change color as it ripens.

Primary cue:

CHECK THE NECK.

==================================================
BOSC
==================================================

Core rule:

BROWN IS NORMAL.

Bosc naturally has cinnamon-brown russeted skin.

Russeting is not damage.

Color change is limited and less reliable than neck feel.

Also note:

Bosc may remain somewhat firmer than other European pears when ready.

Do not force it into exactly the same softness appearance as Bartlett.

==================================================
COMICE
==================================================

Core rule:

TRUST THE NECK.

Color is not the primary ripeness test.

Comice can have delicate skin and superficial marks.

Do not automatically equate every surface mark with internal damage.

Keep wording conservative.

==================================================
ASIAN PEAR EXCEPTION
==================================================

Create a clearly separated editorial section:

ASIAN PEAR?
DIFFERENT RULE.

Core statement:

CRISP CAN BE RIPE.

Teach:

- Asian pears usually stay firm and crisp when ready.
- Do not wait for them to soften like European pears.
- At retail, they are commonly sold ripe / ready to eat.
- Check for overall healthy structure and avoid bruising or localized mushy areas.

Do not apply European neck-softness rule as the primary test.

COLD STORAGE
==================================================

Consumer rule:

COLD SLOWS.
ROOM TEMPERATURE RIPENS.

Teach:

- once ready:
  refrigerator slows further ripening

- ripe European pears:
  generally about 3–5 days refrigerated

Do not make this sound like a guarantee.

Use:

not:
always.
